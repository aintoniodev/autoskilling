# Case (anonymised): "Rally", a habit programme for renewal quoting

Context: a B2B software vendor's renewal sales reps (about 40 reps, several groups, each
with a manager) can quote renewals with an AI quote builder in their CRM, or by hand.
Goal: the AI-built quote becomes the default habit. Team target: 40% of renewals due get
their first quote from the AI tool. The programme runs 8 weeks and then retires.

Principles as designed (each is a hypothesis):

1. Credit per unit of real work: one credit per renewal the rep owns whose quote in play
   is an AI quote the rep actually sent to the customer. Never per click, draft or login.
   A renewal never earns more than one credit. An optional gate: only on-time sends earn.
2. One programme policy object holds every rule, date, threshold and prize, so the
   sponsor's open decisions are config, not code.
3. Milestones scale with each rep's book (30% and 60% of renewals due, with floors and
   caps), and reps are grouped in brackets of similar book size for any comparison.
4. Streak of on-time weeks. Weeks with nothing due are frozen (no change). A rep who
   reports a blocker (the tool couldn't handle a renewal) before quoting by hand keeps the
   streak (excused week). Blocker reports feed a public "You said, we fixed" list.
5. Leaderboard visibility is the sponsor's call and a policy switch: opt-in (default),
   opt-out or everyone; scope within bracket or whole team; top 5 only; hidden below a
   minimum group size. A rep can hide their own card separately.
6. Managers see every rep's activity and score (told to reps at launch).
7. Time-boxed: 8 weeks, then retire. Graduation at a 6-week best streak collapses the
   card to one line; credits continue. Early retirement if the team holds the target.
8. Credits are provisional until a final check 14 days after the end (recalls and
   replacements reverse them, with the reason shown). Swag on send, gift cards after the
   final check.
9. Scores are recomputed nightly from the system of record, never accumulated by hand.
10. Badges for habits (first quote, repeat, regular, on time x4, range of products,
    fixer = reported a blocker that got fixed, coach = named by a peer as their helper).
11. A team finish line (team share vs target) visible to all, and per-group lines
    (target, or starting share + 10 points if lower).
12. A quality alarm when credits rise while recalls or manual replacements also rise.
13. Celebration (confetti + banner) when a credit lands.
