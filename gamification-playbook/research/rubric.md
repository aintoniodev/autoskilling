## Blind design rubric — 10 criteria, 0–10 each

Score the design the agent actually specified, not whether it recites the literature or resembles Rally. No bonus for adding prizes, ranks, streaks or badges; omitting one can earn full credit if the underlying goal and risk are addressed. Use integers: 0 = absent or contrary, 5 = useful but missing a consequential safeguard/test, 10 = decision-ready at the scenario's scale; use intermediate numbers between anchors. Sum to 100 and record a short reason per criterion. A design can score well yet be **not launchable**: flag unmitigated customer/safety risk, invalid core measurement, or coercive employer use separately; these are not cancelled by a high aggregate score. Freeze this rubric and judge instructions before evaluating outputs.

| Criterion | 0 | 5 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Behaviour and value alignment | Counts logins, drafts or game actions only. | Names a real act, but target, credit and outcome are misaligned or underspecified. | Names who does what, on which eligible opportunity and when; aligns success/credit/quality or explicitly reconciles different measures. |
| 2. Workflow feasibility | Assumes motivation is the bottleneck. | Lists barriers or training, but no baseline diagnosis or exception ownership. | Checks real access and friction, demonstrates expected value net of QA, embeds a work cue and safe fallback, and names a blocker owner. |
| 3. Opportunity-adjusted goals and feedback | Raw contest volume, no usable feedback. | Normalised personal or team goals, but weak handling of small books, case mix or next action. | Credible goals tied to baseline/feasibility, clear n/N and next action, with zero opportunities, uneven volume and case mix handled. |
| 4. Quality and anti-gaming | Pays for an easily gamed proxy; no guardrails. | One quality check, but little detection of displacement or no owner/trigger. | Adversarial examples, relevant independent quality and displacement checks, proportionate audit and concrete pause/recovery rule. |
| 5. Incentive and feedback choice | High stakes/use of losses or decorative mechanics without a testable rationale. | Proportionate rewards, but unclear value signal, fairness, timing or verification. | Specific timely feedback; optional additions tied to a mechanism and net value; explains settlement, exceptions and why stakes fit. |
| 6. Lapses and safe re-entry | Punishes leave/outages or creates all-or-nothing failure. | Can restart informally; treats missed, excused and completed events ambiguously. | Clear differentiated rules and an easy next opportunity after a miss, without pressure to log low-quality work or induce misses; streak optional. |
| 7. Privacy and social effects | Mandatory named surveillance or shaming, no meaningful limits. | Discloses visibility or groups peers but purpose, power or harm not addressed. | Purpose-limited views, optional peer display if any, protection against small-group inference, and checks for morale, exclusion and cooperation. |
| 8. Operability and audit | Undefined eligibility/source; no appeal. | A source of record but weak event history or correction rules. | Replayable event/policy versions, independent shadow validation, permissions, transparent corrections and staffed appeals. |
| 9. Durability and exit | Declares success from in-game numbers or assumes a fixed habit clock. | Ends prizes or tracks a vague follow-up. | Plans withdrawal and retained workflow support, measures subsequent eligible opportunities and quality after payout, and defines persistence/stop rules. |
| 10. Learning design | Single before/after claim or cherry-picked enthusiasts. | Credible baseline plus a comparison or operational success criterion, but with gaps. | Isolates incremental value over plain support, handles spillovers/case mix and uncertainty, and predefines outcomes, costs, harms and decisions at feasible sample size. |

**Lowest-cost rollout:** shadow-score representative cases, observe a few live workflows and usability-test the private feedback first. Compare plain feedback to at most one extra mechanic in the initial pilot. Require a separate justification before engineering a ranking service, elaborate rewards or management reporting.

For calibration, have both blind judges score the same non-held-out example before freezing instructions. Resolve differences in **anchor interpretation**, not by changing the rubric after seeing baseline or skill scores. Keep design-eval variance distinct from estimated employee outcomes. For the weakest levers (celebration, complex badge menus, exact thresholds), prefer deletion unless the proposed test isolates meaningful added value.
Weighted total = sum of the 10 criteria (0-100). Use criterion ids c1..c10.
