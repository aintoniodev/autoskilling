# Gamification playbook (v0, from one design, no evidence)

When designing a programme to make people adopt a tool or habit at work, apply these:

1. Reward one unit of real work (an outcome sent to a customer), never clicks or logins. One credit per unit at most.
2. Put every rule, date, threshold and prize in one policy object so open decisions are config.
3. Scale milestones to each person's workload; compare people only within brackets of similar workload.
4. Track a streak of on-time weeks. Weeks with nothing due are frozen. Reporting a blocker before falling back excuses the week; publish what was fixed from the reports.
5. Leaderboard visibility is the sponsor's call: opt-in by default, top 5 only, within brackets, hidden for small groups. People can hide their own card.
6. Managers see everyone's activity; say so at launch.
7. Time-box it (about 8 weeks), graduate people with a long streak, and retire the programme.
8. Keep credits provisional until a final check after the end; reverse gamed ones with a reason.
9. Recompute scores from the system of record every night.
10. Badges for habits: first use, repeat, regular, on time, range, fixed a blocker, coached a peer.
11. Show a team finish line against a target, with softer lines for groups that start low.
12. Raise a quality alarm when credits rise along with recalls or rework.
13. Celebrate each credit visibly.
