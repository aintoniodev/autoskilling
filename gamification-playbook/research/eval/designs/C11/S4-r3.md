## Six-week knowledge-base pilot

Start with a one-week setup. Give six agents across shifts 30 minutes of paid time to try the workflow on real tickets and identify what slows them down. Fix the biggest blockers before rewards start, especially finding the right article, drafting from a ticket, and getting a draft reviewed. Tell the team what changed in response to their feedback. Give all 60 agents 30 minutes of protected practice and schedule time during shifts for article writing.

### What earns credit

Use one rule in the launch message. “A resolved ticket earns one credit when its final resolution uses a relevant, approved knowledge-base article you linked or wrote.”

Use the helpdesk’s existing resolution and article records. Count each ticket ID once. A new article must pass the existing review process and be linked back to the ticket. Let agents resolve tickets while a draft is being reviewed and apply credit to the original resolution week once it’s approved. Where an article can’t safely be shared with a customer, an approved internal article can count if it helped resolve the case.

Show credit as provisional until seven days after resolution. If a ticket reopens during that window, count it once after the final resolution and check that the article still applies. Multiple links add no credit, and a ticket handled entirely through the old workflow earns none.

### What agents see and earn

On resolution, show the agent the article that earned provisional credit, or the reason credit is pending. Give agents a private weekly goal based on their own ticket volume. Start at one qualifying resolution per four eligible tickets, rounded up for anyone who resolves at least one. Every new ticket is a fresh chance to earn credit, and a missed week carries no penalty.

Post the team’s qualifying resolution rate once a week in the existing support channel. Managers see team totals only. Keep individual counts out of performance reviews and keep the current CSAT bonus terms intact.

Announce the reward at launch. If the team reaches **40% of eligible resolutions in weeks 5 and 6 combined** and passes the quality check, all 60 agents earn **one paid hour off**. The support lead recognizes the team at the next huddle, and workforce planning offers staggered slots across shifts.

### Quality and gaming

- **Irrelevant links and duplicate articles.** Each week, a support QA reviewer checks eight randomly sampled credited tickets for relevance, accuracy, and safe handling of customer information. If two fail, the support lead pauses reward qualification and fixes the workflow or guidance before resuming. Any sensitive-information exposure triggers an immediate pause. Agents can ask QA to correct a count privately, and QA checks the ticket record and applies the correction.
- **Delayed resolutions and strained CSAT.** Keep the article field optional and tell agents to close the case as soon as the customer’s issue is resolved. The support lead checks existing team-level CSAT, time-to-resolution, and reopen reports weekly. If those measures worsen, review the affected cases for evidence that the incentive is rushing replies or delaying closure.
- **Easy cases and overused exceptions.** Agree on narrow exclusions before launch, such as spam, merged duplicates, and cases where policy bars any reusable article. Keep hard cases and cases with a missing article in the denominator. QA spot-checks two exclusions each week. Use the existing QA slot and automate the tally so the pilot takes under an hour a week to administer.

### How it ends

Record the four-week pre-pilot qualifying resolution rate using the same eligibility and quality rules. Use the reported 12% as the starting estimate. By week 5, make article suggestions the first option in the resolution panel and put “Create draft” beside them. Keep the option to close without a link.

Stop earning rewards after week 6 and settle pending credit after the seven-day reopen window and any outstanding article reviews. Four weeks after the pilot ends, use the **qualifying resolution rate over the preceding two weeks** to decide what to do. Agree on the bands at launch. Keep the workflow at **30% or higher**, revise it and remeasure if it is **above baseline but below 30%**, and drop the pilot prompt if it is **at or below baseline**. The team reward ends with the pilot.
