## Eight-week knowledge-base adoption programme

**Reward the team for leaving a useful knowledge trail on resolved tickets.** A relevant article link and a newly written article earn the same credit. Agents search first, then link the best existing article, update it if needed, or write a short article if there’s a genuine gap. For new articles awaiting review, the ticket can close with a draft attached. Credit applies to the original resolution week once the draft passes review.

If the team reaches **80% verified coverage across weeks 7 and 8**, with at least **90% of sampled links and articles passing review**, everyone earns two hours of protected, paid learning time. Managers schedule it across shifts within the following month. The existing CSAT bonus keeps its current rules and payout. There are no individual prizes or rankings.

### How it’s measured

- **Coverage.** Count each resolved customer issue once, even if the ticket is reopened or merged. The denominator includes every resolved customer issue. Limit exclusions to predefined administrative closures such as spam and duplicate records. Report exclusions by reason and audit them. A genuine knowledge gap calls for an article or draft.
- **Quality.** Each week, reviewers outside the ticket’s handling chain sample 60 claimed links, spread across queues, shifts, and existing versus newly written articles. A link passes if the article addresses the issue, gives accurate and usable guidance, and contains no customer-sensitive details. Reviewers return failures with a reason and a correction. Weight pass rates by each group’s share of claimed links so oversampling new articles doesn’t skew the result.
- **Verified coverage.** Multiply the share of resolved issues with an approved article attached by the weighted audit pass rate. For example, 90% attachment coverage with a 92% pass rate yields 83% verified coverage. Show pending drafts separately and allow five business days after week 8 for final review.
- **Customer guardrails.** Track CSAT, survey response rate, seven-day reopens, and backlog age by queue against the pre-launch baseline. A CSAT drop of three percentage points or a reopen increase of two points across two consecutive weeks triggers a review. Pool queues with too few survey responses to interpret. If the review finds the workflow is harming service, pause the push and fix it before resuming.

### What people see

At resolution, agents see an article search, an attach action, and a short draft option. Their private view shows recent tickets missing a link, pending drafts, and audit feedback. The weekly team dashboard shows attachment and verified coverage, audit pass rates, pending drafts, and customer guardrails. Leads use one anonymized example each week to show what a good link or article looks like.

### Schedule and ending

Before launch, validate the reported **12% attachment baseline** against the programme’s counting rules and audit a sample for quality. In weeks 1–2, coach agents in the resolution workflow and aim for 35% verified coverage. Aim for 60% in weeks 3–5, then use week 6 to fix recurring gaps ahead of the final two-week measurement window. Give reviewers protected time and a two-business-day turnaround target.

After week 8, close scoring once the review window ends, publish the result, and schedule any earned learning time. The incentive expires on that date. Article linking stays in the resolution workflow, the knowledge-base owner takes over the review queue, and leads check coverage and customer outcomes 30 days later.

### What could go wrong

- **Agents attach generic articles or inflate exclusions.** Sample for relevance and review unusual changes in exclusion rates by queue. Give agents a way to flag a bad audit decision.
- **Agents rush replies, delay closing tickets, or write between shifts.** Give them time to draft during paid shifts and review customer and backlog measures together. Allow tickets to close while drafts are reviewed.
- **Easy queues carry the team while hard queues fall behind.** Check coverage and reviewer turnaround by queue and shift. Direct coaching and review time to teams with the biggest knowledge gaps.
- **The library fills with duplicates or sensitive details.** Credit reuse and updates equally, merge duplicate drafts during review, and check new content before it becomes searchable.
