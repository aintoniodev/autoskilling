## Fix the checklist before launching the reward

Give technicians paid time to test the app with their union reps, including on low-signal sites and on jobs that need a safety hold. Put the checklist first when a technician opens a job, prefill job details, and let technicians save it offline. Confirm that dispatch can reschedule a job on hold and that technicians have a safe fallback when the app fails. Tell crews what they reported and what was fixed. Launch rewards once technicians can use the checklist on paid time under normal field conditions.

## Reward verified safety decisions

Count one safety decision per job that reaches the on-site assessment stage. A job qualifies when the technician completes the digital checklist before starting work, or records a valid safety hold before any work begins. Use the existing job ID, checklist record and job status. For offline jobs, use the recorded completion time when the app syncs. Show the credit as pending until then.

Count duplicate submissions once. If a hold clears, require a valid checklist before work starts and keep the total at one credit. A checklist submitted after work starts earns no credit. During an app outage, technicians follow the existing safety procedure. Record the outage from the existing incident log and show affected jobs separately so technicians can see why those jobs were excluded from their reward target.

Offer **one paid hour off**, earned once. Set each technician’s goal at 80% of their eligible jobs over their first two working weeks, rounded up. A technician with five eligible jobs needs four verified decisions. Show a provisional goal from the roster and settle it on actual eligible jobs, so leave, cancellations and low volume do not make the reward harder to earn. A technician with no eligible jobs gets their next two working weeks. The reward depends only on the technician’s own work, and earned time can be booked after the pilot.

## Show progress where crews already look

Name the paid hour at launch. After each qualifying job, the app shows a private confirmation such as “Pre-job checklist verified. 4 of 5 toward your paid hour off.” The existing crew huddle shows the team’s weekly verified completion rate. Managers see team totals with no individual drill-down. A designated programme steward handles count corrections with the technician, and payroll receives only the information needed to grant earned time. Agree with union reps up front that reward data stays out of performance reviews and discipline.

## Run an eight-week pilot and check what sticks

Give all crews the app fixes and private feedback for two weeks. Start the two-working-week reward window for the first group in week 3 and the second group in week 5. Assign crews to the two start dates by lottery, balancing for baseline completion and job mix. Compare the groups during weeks 3 and 4 to estimate what the reward adds to the fixes and feedback. Keep the checklist first in the job flow after rewards end.

Use one number for the decision in week 10, four weeks after the final scheduled reward window closes: the percentage of eligible jobs with a verified pre-start checklist or valid safety hold. Use the pilot definition to establish a comparable baseline alongside today’s roughly 35%. At **80% or higher**, keep the workflow and feedback and retire the prize. At **60–79%**, fix the biggest remaining barrier and remeasure. **Below 60%**, drop the incentive and redesign the workflow with technicians and union reps.

## Watch for rushed checks, missed credit and pressure to proceed

Each week, the safety lead reviews 10 sampled job records for late completion, skipped required checks and holds that were overridden without clearance. Pause new rewards if **two or more of the 10** fail the check. Treat any unsafe start immediately under the existing safety procedure. The safety lead owns the pause and restart, and the programme steward corrects disputed counts. Review app failures and dispatch pressure with union reps before restarting. Keep the weekly routine to the sample, one team figure and any count corrections.
