# Knowledge base adoption programme

**Reward a resolved ticket with a useful article attached.** Count one credit when an agent resolves an eligible ticket and links an accurate article that would help another agent solve the same issue. A new article counts once it’s published and linked to the ticket. Each team earns a €15 meal voucher per person if it reaches 30% qualifying coverage over its four reward weeks and passes the quality check. Everyone on the team shares the reward, including people on leave. The voucher is independent of the existing CSAT bonus.

**Make the work easier first.** Spend two weeks with agents from different shifts and queues to find out why coverage is at 12%. Give agents 30 minutes of protected, paid practice and fix the biggest friction they identify, such as poor search, missing permissions or a slow publishing queue. Post what changed in the team channel. Put article search and linking in the ticket resolution screen, with a prefilled draft option for genuine gaps. Give agents protected time to write and review articles. Start rewards once agents can complete both paths during a normal shift.

**Measure coverage from existing records.** Divide unique qualifying resolved tickets by all eligible resolved tickets. Use the helpdesk’s ticket status and article links and the knowledge base’s publication status. Start with all resolved tickets and agree narrow exclusions before launch, such as cases that cannot safely be documented and administrative closures already identified by a disposition code. Apply the same rule to the baseline, reward period and follow-up. A ticket resolved the old way remains in the denominator. Before launch, test that two links earn one credit, merged duplicates count once, and a reopened ticket loses its provisional credit until it is resolved again. Allow seven days after each reward window for reopens and pending publications before settling the result.

**Give agents immediate, private feedback.** After resolution, show a confirmation such as “Article linked. One ticket counted toward your goal.” Show pending publication as pending. Give each agent a private target based on their queue’s baseline and expected ticket volume, and size it to the cases they actually handle. Show team coverage and progress toward 30% in the existing support channel. Managers see team totals only. Programme data stays out of performance reviews. Agents can raise a wrong count privately with Support Ops, which checks the ticket record and corrects the tally. After an absence, the next qualifying case counts as usual.

**Stagger the rewards and remove them on schedule.** Give every team the workflow fixes and feedback in weeks 1–2. Assign intact teams to two groups with similar starting coverage and queue mix where possible. The first starts rewards in week 3 and the second in week 5. Each gets four reward weeks. Compare the groups in weeks 3–4 to see what the reward adds while both have the same workflow and feedback. Keep the resolution-screen defaults after the vouchers end. Four weeks after each group finishes, measure coverage for its first four prize-free weeks. Use the change from baseline to decide what to do with the adoption workflow. Keep it at **+15 percentage points or more**, adjust it at **+5 to under +15**, and drop the campaign targets and reporting below **+5**. Apply the quality guardrail before keeping the workflow as designed.

**Watch for irrelevant links, rushed resolutions and publishing delays.** Each week, Support Ops randomly checks 10 credited tickets across queues for relevance, accuracy and safe handling of customer information. If fewer than 9 pass, the Support lead pauses reward payouts, fixes the cause with the knowledge-base owner and resumes after a fresh sample passes. Monitor existing CSAT and reopen rates by queue for signs that agents are rushing to close tickets or that documentation is slowing service. If review delays are holding up valid articles, restore review capacity before judging team results. One Support Ops owner runs the sample, checks the dashboard and posts the weekly team update in under an hour.
