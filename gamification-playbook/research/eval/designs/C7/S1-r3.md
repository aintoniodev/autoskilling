## Pre-job safety checklist pilot

**Reward one completed safety check per job.** A technician earns credit when they complete the digital checklist before starting work, or record a valid safety hold before work starts and follow the existing escalation process. A hold earns the same credit as a cleared job. The launch message names the reward: **one paid hour off for meeting your personal goal during your two-week reward window.** Each technician earns it through their own work and can schedule it with normal shift coverage.

### Before launch

Pair union stewards with technicians from different shifts, routes, and job types. Give them paid time to try the checklist in the field, identify friction such as weak signal or slow sign-in, and confirm that paid time off is a reward they want. Fix the blockers, show crews what changed, and agree with union representatives on counting, access, corrections, and how technicians can take the paid hour. Put the checklist first when a technician opens their next job, with job details pre-filled and safety answers left blank. Give every crew that workflow and private feedback before rewards begin.

### Counting and feedback

Use existing job IDs, checklist or hold records, and the existing job-start timestamp. Start the pilot only once those records can establish the order reliably, including for offline submissions. Test a duplicate, a checklist voided before work starts, a safety hold, and a job completed using the approved paper fallback.

- Count one valid record per job. A duplicate adds zero. A checklist voided before work starts must be replaced before work starts to earn credit. A late checklist adds zero. An approved fallback stays available and is excluded from the personal goal when the app is unavailable.
- Set each technician’s goal before their window using their expected job volume and mix. For example, a technician with 10 eligible jobs might need eight valid checks. Prorate for approved leave and lost opportunities. If someone has no eligible jobs, give them a window when they return. A missed job costs one opportunity, and the next job can still earn credit.
- Confirm privately after each submission whether it counted and show the technician’s progress toward the paid hour. Post team totals in the existing shift huddle or crew channel. Managers see team totals only, with small groups combined to protect privacy. Individual results stay out of performance reviews. A designated administrator fixes disputed counts through the existing support channel and confirms the correction privately.

### Timing and decision

After two weeks with the fixes and feedback in place, start rewards for half the crews. Start the other half two weeks later. Each crew gets two reward weeks. Compare crews during the stagger to see what the paid hour adds over the shared workflow. Award earned time off at a crew huddle, with individual acknowledgment only by choice. Keep the checklist first in the app and retain private feedback when the reward ends.

Use one decision measure: **the share of all in-scope jobs with a valid digital checklist or a documented safety hold before work starts**, measured against the roughly 35% starting point using the same definition. Keep approved paper fallbacks in this denominator so outages stay visible. Four weeks after the last rewards end, keep the workflow as configured at **85% or above**. At **65–84%**, fix the biggest remaining barrier and measure again. Below **65%**, retire the incentive and redesign the workflow with technicians. The safety requirement stays in force throughout.

### What could go wrong

Rushed answers, backdating, and pressure to clear an unsafe job can make the numbers look better while making work less safe. Each week, the safety lead reviews 10 records from the existing job history, covering different job types and including holds. If **two or more of 10** have a material safety defect, pause reward accrual. A single confirmed case of work proceeding despite a required hold triggers an immediate pause. The safety lead and union steward review the cause and agree when to resume. Preserve valid credits already earned. Budget one hour a week for the sample, aggregate report, and routine count corrections.
