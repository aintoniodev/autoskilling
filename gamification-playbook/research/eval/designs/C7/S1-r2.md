## Before the Job

**Fix the workflow first.** Spend two weeks with union stewards and technicians from different shifts, routes and job types. Give them paid time to try the checklist on real jobs, including jobs with poor signal. Fix the blockers they identify, such as lost offline entries or duplicate typing, and show crews what changed. Put the checklist first when a technician opens a job, prefill known details and confirm that it saves offline. Agree with the union on the reward, the counting rule and who can see the data before launch. Keep the approved fallback available and protect technicians’ authority to stop work.

**Reward a verified safety decision.** Launch with one sentence: “Earn 30 minutes of paid time off when you use the app to complete at least 80% of your eligible job checks in either of your two campaign weeks, including any check that correctly puts a job on hold.” Each technician can earn the reward once, independent of crew results. The first week with eligible work starts their two-week window, so leave or a week without assignments does not cost them a chance. A missed check can be recovered on the next job. A steward and a supervisor hand over the paid-time credit in person, privately unless the technician wants recognition, and scheduling makes it usable across shifts.

**Count one decision per job.** Match the app’s saved checklist or hold to the existing work order and job-start record. An offline check counts when it syncs if the app’s recorded completion time precedes the job start. A valid hold counts even if the job never starts. A duplicate, reopened checklist or corrected entry earns no extra credit. A withdrawn or invalid submission loses credit, and a late checklist stays late. The standard applies to jobs even when crews use the approved fallback. During a verified outage, give a documented pre-job fallback check equal reward credit and record it as fallback for the adoption measure. Before launch, replay a duplicate, a withdrawn check, an offline sync, a hold and a fallback job with stewards. Confirm that the existing records support the timing rule before tying pay to it.

**Show progress where people already work.** The app immediately confirms “check saved,” “hold recorded” or “saved offline, awaiting sync.” Technicians see their own qualifying jobs, progress and pending entries. The existing crew briefing shows team totals only, with small crews pooled. Supervisors get no individual drill-down, and campaign data stays out of performance reviews. Technicians can take a missed credit to the campaign coordinator, who checks the existing record, corrects the tally and replies within two working days. Stewards can help resolve disputes.

**Run it in two waves and check what lasts.** Give all crews the fixes, app defaults and private feedback in weeks 1–2. Start two-week reward windows for half the crews in week 3 and the rest in week 5, balancing the waves by baseline use and job mix. Compare the crews during weeks 3–4 to see what the reward adds. Keep the app default and feedback after rewards end. Use one decision metric four weeks after the last reward window closes: the share of eligible work orders with a valid digital safety decision before work, including valid holds. Recompute the roughly 35% baseline with that same rule. At **70% or more**, keep the default and feedback and close the campaign. At **50–69%**, fix the leading remaining barrier and remeasure. **Below 50%**, drop the incentive design and revisit the workflow with technicians and stewards.

**Watch for rushed checks, hidden holds and unfair counts.** The safety lead reviews 10 existing job records each week, including holds and offline or fallback cases when available. Pause reward decisions if two of 10 checks have material errors, or immediately if any job started despite a recorded safety hold. The safety lead decides when to resume with the union steward after the cause is fixed. Review missed credits for patterns by shift, route and connectivity, and correct systemic errors before issuing awards. Automate the tally and reserve a single weekly review slot for sampling, exceptions and payouts.
