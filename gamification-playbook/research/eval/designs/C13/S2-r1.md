## One-working-day code review pilot

**Reward a useful first review.** The team earns a two-hour paid early finish, scheduled around coverage, when at least 80% of eligible PRs receive a qualifying review within one working day for two consecutive weeks and the quality check clears. Everyone in the group earns it, including people who were out. Offer the reward once per group, name it at launch, and have the engineering lead thank the group in person for specific improvements.

**Make reviews easier to pick up.** Before launch, ask a few frequent reviewers and authors where the current 2.6-day median comes from. Give them 30 minutes of protected work time to walk through real examples. Use the fixes they identify to set up a rotating reviewer and backup, protect a daily review block, and put the oldest ready PR first in the existing review queue. Tell the team which changes came from their feedback. Keep the rotation, review block, and queue order after the pilot.

**Count each PR once.** Start the clock when a human-authored PR is marked ready in the repository. One working day means eight hours on the team’s agreed work calendar, excluding weekends and team holidays. The first formal approval or request for changes from someone other than the author stops the clock. Use existing repository timestamps and review states. A late assignment, reassignment, or push to the branch leaves the original clock in place. Drafts are outside the measure until they first become ready.

Before launch, walk through four cases with the team. Two reviews on one PR earn one count. A review dismissed before the deadline gives way to the next qualifying review, if one arrives in time. A review delivered in chat earns credit when the reviewer submits the decision on the PR within the window. A PR closed or returned to draft without a qualifying review stays in the denominator, with genuinely cancelled work eligible for a correction by the pilot owner. Group results by the week the PR became ready and publish them once every PR in that week has had its full window.

**Show people what they need to act.** Use the existing review notification to confirm the PR and whether its first response met the window. Each reviewer’s private goal is to answer the PRs assigned during their rotation within the window or hand them to the backup early enough to act. A missed day starts again with the next PR. Post each group’s weekly percentage and PR count in the team’s usual channel. Managers get aggregate results only. Keep individual counts out of dashboards and performance reviews.

**Run a staggered pilot.** Use two existing review groups if possible. Give both the same workflow fixes and feedback for two weeks. Then offer the reward to the first group for four weeks, with the second group starting two weeks later. Measure the on-time percentage from the four weeks before the fixes as the baseline. During the stagger, compare the groups’ changes from their own baseline to see whether the reward appears to add anything. Shared reviewers or low PR volume can make that comparison inconclusive.

**Watch for rushed reviews and quiet queue-jumping.** Each week, a rotating senior engineer uses existing PR access to sample five counted reviews across the groups, including fast approvals and larger changes. Check whether each decision is defensible from the diff, checks, and review discussion. If two of five are materially superficial, the engineering lead pauses the reward and fixes the workflow before restarting it. The pilot owner corrects disputed counts in the next weekly total. Record quality findings in aggregate and budget 45 minutes a week for sampling, corrections, and posting.

**Decide three weeks after each group’s reward ends.** Use the same on-time percentage over those three prize-free weeks. At **80% or higher**, keep the workflow as standard. Below 80% but **at least 10 percentage points above baseline**, adjust review coverage and remeasure. Below both thresholds, drop the incentive programme and use the late PRs to identify what still blocks timely review. The reward expires after four weeks in every case.
