# Four-week knowledge-base adoption programme

**Reward a resolved ticket with a useful article.** “A resolved ticket earns one credit when you link an article that would help someone handle the same issue, or write a new one that passes the usual knowledge-base review.” Existing articles and new articles earn the same credit. Internal articles count for issues that need internal guidance. Keep the existing CSAT bonus and its rules in place.

**Fix the close flow before launch.** Give six agents across shifts 30 minutes of paid time to try the workflow on recent tickets. Ask where search fails, where linking adds work, and where writing feels risky. Fix the main friction points before starting the reward and post what changed in the team channel. Give every agent 20 minutes of covered, paid practice and let agents use existing knowledge-base time to write articles that need more work.

**Measure one outcome per ticket.** Use the ticket system’s resolution records and knowledge-base links to report the percentage of resolved tickets with a qualifying article. Start from the current 12% and verify the prior four weeks using the same counting rules. Before launch, run these cases through the report:

- Two links on one ticket earn one credit. Several tickets can use the same article when it fits each issue.
- Reopening a ticket or removing its only qualifying link reverses the credit. A subsequent resolution can restore it once.
- A new article counts once approved. Attribute it to the week the ticket was resolved so review delays don’t penalize agents.
- Tickets closed without an article stay in the denominator. If there’s no reusable guidance or a link would expose sensitive information, agents can skip it and use the existing exception reason if one fits. Review those reasons for gaps in the tool or guidance.

**Show progress where agents already work.** Confirm a link in the close panel with “Article linked · 1 ticket counted.” Show newly written articles as pending until approved. Give each agent a private count and a suggested goal of one qualifying ticket per five resolutions over the four weeks, scaled to their volume. For someone who resolves fewer than five tickets, suggest trying it on their next suitable case. A missed week leaves prior progress intact. Post the team’s cumulative rate each week in its usual channel. Managers see team totals and use individual records when a specific case needs coaching or correction.

**Make the team milestone reachable.** Aim for **20% of resolved tickets over four weeks**, up from 12%. If the team reaches it and passes the quality check, everyone gets one extra 30-minute paid break, scheduled across shifts to preserve queue coverage. Personal goals guide practice. The reward depends on the team result.

**Catch shortcuts and protect service quality.** The existing QA lead samples 10 credited tickets a week across shifts and checks whether each article is relevant, accurate, and safe to share with its intended audience. Watch for generic links, duplicate articles, and tickets closed before the customer’s issue is solved. If fewer than 9 of 10 pass, or any article exposes restricted information, the support ops lead pauses reward accrual, corrects affected credits, and resumes after a fresh sample passes. Use the existing CSAT report to check whether scores fall below their usual range. If they do, review the sampled tickets for rushed closes or delayed replies and pause accrual if the workflow is contributing. Budget 30 minutes for sampling and 15 minutes for the report and team update each week.

**End the reward after week four.** Before it ends, put relevant article suggestions first in the ticket close panel and keep a one-click way to link or skip. For the next four weeks, track the same adoption rate alongside CSAT and sampled article quality. Compare with the four-week pre-launch baseline and use the final two weeks to judge whether adoption is holding. Keep the close-panel default if adoption stays above baseline and quality and CSAT hold. If adoption falls back or service quality slips, use the sampled tickets and agent feedback to decide what to change or remove.
