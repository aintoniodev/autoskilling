# Knowledge-base adoption pilot

**Give agents a faster way to use the knowledge base.** In the first two weeks, ask agents from different queues and shifts to walk through tickets where finding or writing an article is slow. Give everyone 30 minutes of protected, paid practice. Fix the biggest blockers, tell agents what changed, and put article search and suggestions in the resolution screen. Agents confirm a suggested article, link a different one, or save a reusable article from the ticket. Keep resolution available when there’s a legitimate exception, such as a privacy-sensitive case. Aim to get from today’s 12% to 30% in the pilot, with article coverage on every applicable ticket as the longer-term standard.

**Reward useful coverage on resolved tickets.** Announce at launch that everyone on a team earns one hour of scheduled paid time off if the team reaches 30% coverage across its four reward weeks and clears the quality checks. The support lead announces the result at a team huddle, and workforce scheduling makes the hour available across shifts. Keep the existing CSAT bonus unchanged.

The rule agents see is **“A resolved ticket counts once when it links an article that helps explain or reproduce the fix, including a new article that passes the usual publishing review.”** Use existing ticket resolution and article-link records. Divide qualifying unique tickets by all unique tickets resolved in the period so the rate stays comparable with the 12% baseline. Record exceptions through existing resolution reasons where available. A ticket closed without an article stays in the denominator. Multiple links earn one credit, a removed link loses credit, and reopening and reclosing the same ticket cannot earn a second credit. Confirm these cases against actual records before launch.

**Show agents their own progress and managers the team total.** At resolution, show a specific confirmation such as “Password reset guide linked. This ticket counts.” Show pending review for new articles. In the existing team dashboard, agents see their own count and rate and a suggested goal based on their ticket volume, such as six useful links over roughly 20 resolutions. Everyone sees the team’s progress toward 30%. Managers get team totals without individual drill-down, and pilot counts stay out of performance reviews. A missed week has no penalty. The next qualifying ticket counts as usual.

**Check that customers and the knowledge base are benefiting.** Each week, the knowledge-base owner samples 10 credited tickets per team, or all if fewer, and checks whether the article is relevant, accurate, safe to share, and reusable. Pause reward qualification if fewer than 9 of 10 pass, or if any sample exposes customer data. The support operations lead owns pause and restart decisions and corrects disputed counts. Watch CSAT and seven-day reopen rates alongside coverage. Investigate a five-percentage-point CSAT decline or a three-point rise in reopens against the team’s run-in baseline, checking survey volume and ticket mix before deciding whether to pause. Irrelevant links and duplicate articles can inflate coverage. Rushed closures and delayed replies can hurt customers. Use the sample and customer measures to catch both.

**Stagger the reward and check what lasts.** Give all 60 agents the workflow fixes and feedback in weeks 1–2, and measure coverage during a shared run-in in weeks 3–4. Assign existing teams to two cohorts, balancing for queue mix and baseline coverage. Run four reward weeks for each cohort, starting in weeks 5 and 7. Compare their changes during weeks 5–6 to estimate what the reward adds. When each cohort finishes, end the prize and keep article suggestions and feedback in the resolution flow.

Use the **percentage-point change in coverage from the post-fix run-in to the four weeks after prizes end** to decide what to do next. Keep the workflow and feedback without recurring prizes if coverage holds at least 10 points above the run-in and quality checks pass. If it holds 5–9 points higher, fix the biggest remaining friction and remeasure. Below 5 points, retire the incentive and investigate why agents still skip the article step. Resolve any quality pause before calling the pilot a success.
