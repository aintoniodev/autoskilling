## Pre-job safety checklist pilot

**Reward a completed pre-job safety check.** During the pilot, every technician on a crew that reaches 70% verified digital checklist completion over its four reward weeks earns one paid hour off, scheduled with normal coverage. Agree on the reward and scheduling with union stewards and technicians before launch, announce it at the regular safety huddle, and have the field-service lead recognize each qualifying crew in person. Everyone on the crew shares the reward, including people who were on leave or had few assigned jobs.

**Make the app usable first.** Spend two weeks with technicians and stewards testing the checklist on paid time across shifts, job types, and low-signal sites. Fix the blockers they find, especially offline access and time pressure at dispatch, and show the fixes at the next huddle. Give every crew the fixes, paid practice, and private feedback before rewards begin. Keep the approved fallback available when the app fails, and preserve technicians’ authority to stop unsafe work.

**Count one check per eligible job.** Use the existing job ID, checklist submission time, and recorded job-start time. A fully completed digital checklist earns credit if it was submitted before work began. A documented pre-start safety stop also qualifies when the digital checklist records the hazard before the stop and the job record confirms the hold. Count each job once, including reopened jobs. Exclude jobs canceled before a technician begins the safety check. For offline jobs, use the captured completion time if the app already records it reliably. Record approved fallbacks through the existing process and show confirmed app outages separately so they can be removed from the reward denominator. Keep fallback jobs in the overall digital adoption measure. Test duplicates, late submissions, voided forms, offline sync, and safety stops before launch.

**Show people their progress.** The app confirms which job counted after submission or sync. Each technician can see their own eligible jobs and completions, with a personal goal of seven in ten over the reward window, scaled to actual job volume. The regular huddle shows only the crew total and progress toward the shared milestone. Managers get crew totals. Reporting has no individual drill-down, and programme data stays out of performance reviews. A missed week leaves the next job as another chance to count. Technicians can raise discrepancies privately through the existing support channel. The pilot owner checks the job record and corrects crew totals.

**Stagger the rewards and make the app the default.** After the two shared setup weeks, assign comparable crews to start rewards in pilot week 3 or week 5. Each crew gets four reward weeks. Compare the crews’ change from their shared baseline during the two weeks when only the first group has rewards. Before a crew’s rewards end, make the checklist the first screen on opening a job, with job details prefilled and safety answers left for the technician. Check adoption again four weeks after each crew’s reward window closes. Use one decision measure, the percentage of all eligible jobs with a verified pre-job digital check. Keep the app default at 70% or higher, fix the remaining barriers and recheck at 50–69%, and drop the incentive approach below 50% while maintaining the safety-check requirement.

**Watch for rushed checks and pressure to start.** In the existing safety review, the safety lead samples five credited jobs a week for meaningful answers and evidence that the check happened before work. If two of the five fail that standard, the safety lead pauses rewards, reviews the cause with a steward, and resumes after the issue is fixed and the next sample passes. Use the existing incident process for any report that someone was pressured to work through a hazard or falsify a check. Include a private route through the steward for reporting pressure or unfair counts. Keep administration under an hour a week by generating totals from existing records and folding the sample into the scheduled review.
