# Close the Loop: eight-week knowledge-base pilot

**Goal:** Move from 12% of resolved tickets linked to an article to 80% in weeks 7–8, without making support worse. The everyday expectation is to check for a relevant article on every resolution. The existing CSAT bonus stays exactly as it is. Pilot scores do not affect pay or performance ratings.

### What earns credit

One provisional credit per unique customer issue resolved and communicated to the customer. At close, the resolving agent either links a current article that supports the actual resolution or submits an original article tied to that ticket. A draft counts only after a knowledge-base editor approves and publishes it. Reusing an article earns exactly as much as writing one. No credit for clicks, extra links, duplicate tickets, or reopening and closing the same issue again.

If nothing fits, the agent records a knowledge gap before closing and sends the customer the best available answer. Never hold a resolution for editorial approval or force an irrelevant link. Give editors two business days to review drafts; route sensitive cases to approved internal articles and keep customer data out of the knowledge base.

### How it is measured

Support Ops publishes one versioned policy before launch with the calendar, eligibility, scoring, thresholds, visibility, rewards, audit and appeal rules. Every night, rebuild scores from helpdesk resolution events and knowledge-base records. Attribute each issue once to the resolving agent and original close week. Exclude spam, test tickets and merged duplicates. Keep genuine unresolved knowledge gaps and blocked cases in the coverage denominator, with separate reason counts, so exceptions cannot inflate adoption.

An on-time week meets the target for that agent’s actual workload: **40% of eligible resolutions in weeks 1–2, 60% in weeks 3–4 and 80% in weeks 5–8**, rounded up to whole tickets. The link or draft must be recorded by close; an approved draft is credited back to that week. Weeks with no eligible resolutions freeze the streak. A blocker reported before using a fallback freezes a week if a lead confirms it caused the missed target. Frozen weeks neither extend nor break the streak. Publish what was fixed from blocker reports every Friday.

### What people see and earn

- **Agents** see a private confirmation for every provisional credit, plus their ticket-level record, reversals, weekly target and streak. Badges recognize first credit, five credits, four qualifying weeks, two on-time weeks in a row, coverage across three issue categories, a verified blocker fix and a confirmed peer coaching session. Badges add no credits.
- **Teams** see aggregate coverage against their finish line. Teams starting below 15% get an interim target of 50% by week 4 and a finish line of 70%; the org-wide goal stays 80%. Any team that reaches its line without an unresolved quality alarm gets a team celebration during paid time.
- **Managers** see everyone’s individual activity and audit results; say this at launch. Recommend no leaderboard. If the sponsor enables it, it is opt-in, shows only the top five by coverage within workload brackets set from pre-pilot ticket volumes, and hides brackets with fewer than ten opted-in agents. Agents can hide their card at any time.

### What could go wrong

**Link stuffing, duplicate articles and easy-ticket cherry-picking.** QA samples 30 credited resolutions weekly across queues and shifts, plus suspicious patterns. Check relevance, accuracy, privacy and whether the customer’s issue was actually resolved. Keep normal routing in place and inspect changes in case mix and transfers. Reverse invalid credits with a specific reason.

**More credits, worse support.** Compare weekly coverage, seven-day reopens, QA rework, resolution time and CSAT with the four weeks before launch, by queue and case mix. If coverage rises while reopens or rework rise by at least 2 percentage points, or CSAT falls by at least 3 points, QA reviews the cases and survey response volume before drawing conclusions. Pause recognition in affected queues during the review. Pause immediately for a confirmed serious privacy or safety error. Never suppress surveys, pressure customers for ratings or change the CSAT bonus.

### How it ends

Stop earning credits after week 8. Allow two business days for pending drafts, then complete the final audit after the last seven-day reopen window. Publish reasoned reversals privately and allow three business days to appeal before finalizing. Anyone with a verified streak of six on-time weeks graduates with a certificate and one hour of protected learning time.

Retire points, badges and any leaderboard. Keep the closeout prompt, editorial queue and aggregate coverage reporting. Four weeks later, check whether coverage held without recognition and whether support quality stayed steady.
