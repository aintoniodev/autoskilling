# Before the Job: an eight-week safety checklist pilot

**Goal:** Raise verified pre-job digital checklist completion from about 35% to 80% in weeks 7–8 without rushing checks, discouraging hazard reports, or pressuring anyone to start an unsafe job.

**Before launch.** Operations, Safety, and union representatives agree on one published pilot policy covering dates, eligible jobs, scoring, fallback, targets, recognition, data access and retention, audits, and appeals. Provide company devices, offline access, accessible training, and paid time to do the check. Recognition is optional; the safety check is not. Pilot scores don’t affect pay, assignments, performance reviews, or discipline. Existing safety procedures still apply.

## What earns credit

Award **one provisional credit per eligible work order** to the designated technician when they complete a job-specific checklist and record the decision in the work order before work begins. A valid **stop-work decision** logged and routed to dispatch or Safety earns the same credit as a safe-to-proceed decision, even if the job never starts. No credit for opening the app, duplicate submissions, backfilling after starting, or completing the job itself.

The app confirms each submission privately: “Pre-job check recorded · 1 provisional credit.” If the device or app fails, use the approved offline or paper check before starting. Report the blocker to dispatch before switching to paper when possible; if communications are down, record it locally and report once reachable. **Never delay a safety stop to log a blocker.**

## How it’s measured

Recompute scores nightly from dispatch work orders, checklist records, and independently recorded job starts or safety holds. Accept delayed offline sync when the original completion time can be verified; send ambiguous records for review rather than guessing. The adoption rate is verified on-time digital checks divided by all work orders that reached the pre-job safety decision point. Exclude jobs cancelled before that point; retain safety holds, paper fallbacks, and missing checks in the denominator. Show paper fallback separately so low app adoption isn’t mistaken for skipped safety checks. Recalculate the four-week baseline on the same basis.

A streak advances when all of a technician’s due checks that week qualify, regardless of whether they had two jobs or twenty. A week with no checks due freezes the streak. If a documented, approved fallback is the only reason a week falls short, freeze it rather than advancing or breaking it. Offer private badges for first use, repeat use, three qualifying weeks, six on-time weeks, checks across two assigned job types, helping resolve an app blocker, and coaching a peer on paid time. Badges don’t add credits.

## What people see

Technicians see their own jobs, credits, exceptions, streak, and correction route. Crews see their aggregate progress, not names or ranks. The sponsor keeps individual leaderboards **off** for this pilot. Publish crew results only for groups of at least ten technicians; roll smaller groups up. Crews starting below 35% get an interim finish line of **20 percentage points above baseline**, with 80% still the company goal. Neither target gates anyone’s reward.

At launch, state plainly that authorised operations and safety managers can see every technician’s pilot activity in their remit, even if the technician opts out of recognition. Publish a weekly **“You reported / we fixed”** update without naming reporters.

## What could go wrong, and how it ends

- **Rubber-stamping, backdating, or dispatch pressure.** Safety audits a random 5% of credited jobs each week against job details and start records, plus flagged anomalies. If adoption rises while safety-related callbacks or rework per 100 jobs also rise for two weeks, pause recognition and review with the union. A verified unsafe start triggers immediate review. Don’t treat more hazard or near-miss reports as failure; recognition must never depend on low reporting. [OSHA worker participation guidance](https://www.osha.gov/safety-management/worker-participation)
- **Unequal access or fear of surveillance.** Review fallback rates by depot, shift, and device; fix access problems before blaming adoption. Offer a confidential route to Safety or a union representative for pressure, reporting concerns, or score disputes.
- **Closeout.** Stop earning credits after week eight. In week nine, reconcile offline records and audits, reverse invalid credits with a written reason, and allow five working days for appeals before finalising. Privately recognise anyone with a six-week qualifying streak; no cash, quotas, or competitive prizes. Retire the points, badges, and dashboards. Keep the check and fallback in normal operations, delete pilot-only individual scores 30 days after appeals close, and remeasure aggregate adoption and audit quality four weeks later.
