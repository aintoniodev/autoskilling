# Knowledge base adoption pilot

**Reward a resolved ticket with one relevant, approved knowledge-base article attached.** Agents can link an existing article or write one that is approved and attached before the ticket closes. Internal articles count for cases where customer-facing guidance would be inappropriate. Keep the existing CSAT bonus as it is. At launch, announce an employer-paid team lunch on work time for each team that reaches 40% coverage during its four-week reward window and passes the quality check. Every team can earn it.

**Make it easier to do during a normal shift.** Before launch, have agents from different shifts walk through recent tickets with the knowledge-base owner. Fix the search, permissions, and approval delays they identify. Give agents protected time during paid shifts to practice and write articles, and show them what changed based on their feedback. Put article suggestions and a link picker in the ticket resolution screen for all teams before rewards begin. Keep resolution available when no suitable article exists so agents can close the ticket promptly and flag the gap.

**Use the ticket system’s existing records.** Divide qualifying tickets by all resolved support tickets, excluding spam, tests, and merged duplicates using existing dispositions. Count each ticket ID once, even if it has several links or changes owners. Credit the resolving agent and their team. Show credit immediately as provisional and finalize it after 72 hours. If a ticket reopens within that window or its link is removed, withdraw the credit. A reopened ticket can earn credit again when it is resolved with a valid article, once in total. An unrelated link, a draft, or a resolution without an article earns no credit. Test those cases against the report before launch.

**Give agents private feedback and show teams their progress.** On resolution, tell the agent whether the ticket earned credit and why. In the existing support dashboard, show each agent their own qualifying tickets and a personal goal of four in ten resolutions, scaled to their actual ticket volume. A missed week carries no penalty. Post team coverage weekly in the channel agents already use. Managers see team totals only. Keep individual figures out of performance reviews and let agents send ticket IDs privately to the pilot owner for count corrections.

**Check for shortcuts and service degradation.** Each week, the support QA lead samples 10 credited tickets across the teams earning rewards. Check that the article would help solve the issue and that it contains no customer data. If fewer than nine pass, the QA lead pauses rewards and checks whether irrelevant links, duplicate articles, or resolution shortcuts are driving the misses. A customer-data exposure triggers an immediate pause and the existing incident process. Check the existing team-level CSAT report each week. If a team’s rolling two-week CSAT drops by five percentage points or more from its pre-pilot baseline, with at least 30 responses in both periods, the support lead pauses that team’s reward and reviews ticket mix and QA findings before restarting. The pilot owner corrects count errors and the knowledge-base owner fixes article or workflow problems. Budget 30 minutes for sampling and 25 for reporting, CSAT, and disputes each week.

**End rewards after four weeks per team.** Give everyone the same fixes and feedback for two weeks, then randomly assign existing teams to two start dates three weeks apart. Compare the change in coverage during those three weeks while only the first group has rewards. Deliver earned lunches after each team’s window and remove reward progress from the dashboard. Keep article suggestions, private confirmation, and protected writing time in place.

Four weeks after the last reward window ends, use the **share of resolved tickets with a qualifying article** over the preceding two weeks to decide what stays. At **40% or higher**, keep the workflow without prizes. At **25–39%**, fix the largest remaining barrier and measure again four weeks later. **Below 25%**, retire the campaign and return to improving article coverage and search through normal support operations.
