## 1. Diagnosis

Pilot on three wards. Low kiosk use could reflect poor access, extra work, or a workflow nurses have yet to try. Before launch, the ward nursing leads shadow a routine hand-hygiene moment with a kiosk on the nurse’s existing route and an urgent-care moment where stopping would delay care. On the routine case, test whether logging takes 10 seconds or less and gives nurses something they value, such as replacing duplicate manual reporting. Confirm the benefit with day and night staff.

Hand hygiene and patient care always come first. If the kiosk is unavailable or logging would interrupt care, skip the entry and continue the usual workflow. The nurse informatics lead owns kiosk blockers, responds within one working day, and posts fixes in the shift huddle. Defer the incentive if most routine opportunities are blocked or nurses see no benefit after fixes.

## 2. Credit and measurement

After completing hand hygiene at an eligible routine moment, the nurse records the moment type at the next safe break within 15 minutes. One completed moment can earn one team credit. For the reward, Infection Prevention counts only entries it can match one-to-one to a completed action in its independent monthly audit, using a temporary staff code, ward, moment type, and timestamp. Ambiguous matches earn no credit. Record no patient identifiers.

**Kiosk adoption** is verified entries divided by independently observed, completed hand-hygiene actions eligible for kiosk logging. **Observed hand-hygiene compliance** is completed actions divided by all observed hand-hygiene opportunities, including those ineligible for kiosk logging. Define exclusions before baseline and report their frequency. Sample across day, night, and weekend shifts using the same approach throughout. Observation can itself change behavior. [WHO hand-hygiene observation guidance](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144056/)

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Routine action completed and matched to a timely entry | One credit |
| Duplicate entry | One credit total |
| Entry withdrawn or observer records hygiene was missed | Zero credit |
| Entry submitted after 15 minutes or timestamp edited | Zero credit. Use the original server timestamp. |
| Hygiene completed using the usual workflow without a kiosk entry | Counts toward observed compliance. Zero kiosk credit. |
| Eligible completed action omitted from the kiosk | Stays in the adoption denominator. Zero kiosk credit. |

## 3. What nurses see

- **Invitation at shift huddle and on the kiosk:** “After your next routine hand-hygiene moment, tap the moment type when it’s safe. You’ll see immediately that your entry saved. If care needs you, skip the kiosk. The team reward ends March 31.”
- **Confirmation:** “After-patient-contact moment saved. You can correct this entry before the end of your shift.”
- **Private progress:** “You logged a routine moment on 2 of your 3 eligible shifts this week. On your next shift, try the first routine moment you can safely log.” Offer a practice goal of one entry per shift with a safe opportunity, adjusted for shifts worked. A shift with no safe opportunity carries no goal.
- **Restart:** “Start again with your next safe routine moment. There’s nothing to make up.”

## 4. Visibility

Nurses see their own entries and practice progress. The staff huddle board shows each ward’s verified adoption rate against its own baseline after each monthly audit. Managers see the same aggregates. Suppress results from groups with fewer than five contributing nurses or where someone could be identified. Only the designated reviewer may access individual records for a correction or dispute. Log that access, expire it after resolution, and prohibit disciplinary reuse.

## 5. Reward

Offer a hospital-funded ward meal, with equivalent provision for every shift and open to all ward staff. Test the choice with nurses across shifts. Each ward earns it independently by reaching **at least 80% verified adoption and closing at least 20% of its gap to 100%**, with observed compliance meeting the safety rule below. Announce verified results and the workflow fixes staff requested at the final huddle.

## 6. Operator card

Assume Infection Prevention and Privacy approve temporary matching, kiosk history preserves edits, and monthly audits can cover **at least 50 eligible completed opportunities per ward per phase**. Validate matching during baseline before announcing the reward. Nurses can request corrections privately through the ward educator within five working days of an entry. Budget one hour per ward per week for the educator and informatics lead, plus two hours per ward per monthly audit for Infection Prevention matching and review.

The Infection Prevention lead pauses the incentive and reward adjudication if a monthly compliance result falls **five percentage points or more** below baseline, or immediately if logging delays care. Investigate before resuming. Continue routine hand hygiene, audits, and safe fallback throughout.

## 7. End and decision

**January 2027:** baseline with the supported kiosk workflow and no reward. **February–March:** incentive. Before March 31, put the one-tap logging screen first on kiosks along existing routes. **April–May:** keep that default and private confirmation, with no rewards.

Expand only if every ward meets its adoption target during both the incentive and follow-up, clears the safety rule, and meets the audit sample minimum. Revise and retest if adoption fades or sampling falls short. Stop the incentive if care disruption or a compliance decline persists after fixes. Treat the baseline comparison as a feasibility result.

## 8. Main risks

- Duplicate or invented entries inflate activity. Reward only independently matched work.
- Kiosk detours delay care or burden some shifts. Keep the safe fallback and review exclusions by shift.
- Staff feel watched or change behavior during audits. Restrict access and interpret observed compliance within the sampled sessions.
