## 1. Diagnosis

Reported adoption is 12%. The cause is unknown. Before launch, have two agents walk through a password-reset ticket with a usable article and a new billing issue with no article and no permission to publish. Time both paths and check whether article work delays resolution. The Support Ops lead owns blockers and responds within two business days. Agents resolve blocked tickets normally. Launch only if most tickets are supported, both paths are worthwhile for agents, and some agents simply haven’t tried them.

## 2. Credit rule

When an agent resolves a ticket, earn one credit by linking an accurate, approved article that explains the resolution, or publishing and linking a reusable article through the usual approval process. Allow seven calendar days after resolution for approval and linking. Exclude only predeclared categories such as spam, merged duplicates, and cases where policy prohibits documentation. Track tool and access blockers separately and keep them in the denominator. The adoption rate is credited tickets divided by all eligible resolved tickets.

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Relevant approved article linked | One credit |
| Second link or duplicate ticket | No additional credit |
| Ticket reopens or article is withdrawn | Remove pending credit. Reassess reopened tickets after final resolution. |
| Late link or edited due date | Use the recorded final resolution timestamp. Links after seven days earn zero. |
| Ticket resolved the old way | Eligible, zero credit |
| Ticket omitted from the programme report | Reconcile against the resolution export. Eligible, zero until verified. |

## 3. Worker copy

- **Invitation at resolution** “Link the article you used so the next agent can reuse your answer. If it’s missing, submit the fix as an article. If you’re blocked, resolve the ticket as usual and flag it here. The rally ends February 28.”
- **Confirmation** “Article linked to ticket #4821. That’s 2 of your 3 this week.” For submissions, show “Awaiting approval” until they qualify.
- **Progress** “2 of 3 this week. On your next eligible ticket, link the article that explains the fix.” Set the goal at 30% of the agent’s eligible volume, rounded up. With one or two opportunities, invite one try. With zero, show no goal.
- **Restart** “Start with your next eligible ticket. Last week’s progress stays recorded.”

## 4. Visibility

Agents see their own progress. The existing support channel shows the team’s verified rate against baseline, without names or identifiable breakdowns. Managers see the same aggregate. Support Ops logs individual access for requested coaching or corrections and closes access when the case is resolved. Rally data stays out of performance management and the existing CSAT bonus.

## 5. Reward

Test the offer with four agents before launch. If the team reaches 30% verified adoption and clears the quality check, everyone gets 30 minutes of paid flex time, including agents with few opportunities. Use the same channel to report which agent-raised blockers were fixed.

## 6. Operator card

Assume ticket and KB exports contain timestamps, reopen history, and article approval status, and that paid time is approved. Support Ops reconciles exports and handles corrections privately by ticket ID. An independent QA reviewer samples eight credited tickets weekly across linked and new articles. Pause reward qualification if two fail relevance or accuracy, or one exposes customer data. Fix the issue and pass a fresh sample before resuming. Budget 50 minutes weekly across both roles, plus blocker fixes.

## 7. End and decision

Measure baseline February 1–28, 2027. Run March 1–28, settle credits and award by April 4, then measure April 5–May 2 with workflow fixes and private feedback still in place. Scale if rally adoption reaches 30%, follow-up holds at 25% and at least 10 percentage points above baseline, quality passes, and CSAT falls by no more than two points with comparable response rates. Change the workflow if adoption fades or approvals bottleneck. Stop if quality or CSAT problems persist. Treat the baseline comparison as feasibility evidence.

## 8. Risks

Agents attach loosely related articles. Article work delays replies and puts CSAT pay under pressure. Adoption falls once paid time ends.
