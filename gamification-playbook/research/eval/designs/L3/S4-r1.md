## 1. Diagnosis

Agents currently link or write an article on 12% of resolved tickets. The cause is unknown. Before launch, Support Ops walks through one ticket with a usable article and one with a knowledge gap, alongside agents. Check access, time to find or publish an article, and whether the extra step delays resolution. Fix access and approval bottlenecks within two business days. Keep normal resolution available when the tool or publishing process is blocked. Launch only if most tickets have a workable path and agents can use it without materially slowing support.

## 2. What earns credit

At ticket resolution, the resolving agent earns **one credit per ticket** for linking an approved article that addresses the actual issue or writing an article that passes normal editorial review and is linked to the ticket. Pending articles earn provisional credit. Use the first resolution timestamp to assign the ticket to a week.

The team metric is verified credits divided by all unique resolved tickets with a supported knowledge-base path. Exclude documented blockers and cases unsuitable for a reusable article, such as sensitive one-off cases. Show the exclusion count and coverage across **all** resolved tickets so changes in eligibility stay visible.

| Case | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Relevant article linked at resolution | One credit |
| Two links, duplicate events, or a second closure | One credit maximum per ticket |
| Ticket reopens or article is withdrawn | Remove pending credit until the ticket is resolved with a valid article. Correct reporting without taking back earned rewards. |
| Due date moves or article approval arrives late | Due-date edits have no effect. Approval before final review counts in the original resolution week. |
| Agent closes the ticket the old way | Eligible ticket stays in the denominator with zero credit. |
| Ticket is missing from the KB report | Reconcile to the ticketing export. Include the eligible ticket and verify any missing credit before payout. |

## 3. What agents see

- **Invitation at resolution.** “Found a fix you could reuse? Link the article before closing. If there’s a gap, submit the fix for review so the next agent can find it. If the KB is blocked, resolve the ticket as usual and flag it. The team reward runs February 8 through March 7, 2027.”
- **Confirmation.** “Article linked to ticket 4821. One credit recorded.” For a submission, show “Credit pending editorial approval.”
- **Progress.** “2 of 3 this week. On your next eligible ticket, look for an existing article first.” Set a private goal around 30% of each agent’s recent eligible volume. For agents with one or two opportunities, suggest one link. With zero, show “No eligible tickets this week.”
- **Restart.** “Your next eligible ticket is a fresh chance. Start by checking for an existing article.”

## 4. Visibility

Agents see their own progress. The existing weekly support update shows the 60-person team’s coverage against baseline, with names and identifying small groups omitted. Managers see aggregates. Support Ops may open individual records for corrections, disputes, or requested coaching, with logged, time-limited access and no disciplinary reuse. Explain access at launch.

## 5. Reward

If the team reaches **30% verified coverage** across the four weeks and clears the quality gate, everyone gets **one paid hour of their choice**, scheduled around queue coverage. Include agents with few eligible tickets. Test the milestone and reward with a few agents before launch. Use the weekly update to show which blockers their reports helped fix. Keep the CSAT bonus terms unchanged.

## 6. Operator card

Support Ops owns the pilot, uses existing ticket and KB exports, and takes private corrections through the existing support channel. Budget **50 minutes weekly**, including independent QA that samples six credited tickets and two exclusions for relevance, accuracy, and sensitive content. Support Ops pauses reward accrual and payout if two sampled credits fail in a week or one exposes sensitive information. Fix the issue and pass a fresh sample before resuming. Launch assumes auditable ticket and article history, paid-time approval, and QA capacity.

## 7. End and decision

Baseline January 11–February 7, 2027. Reward February 8–March 7. Follow up March 8–April 4 with workflow fixes and private feedback retained. Recalculate the reported 12% using the same eligibility rules. Scale if coverage reaches 30% during rewards and holds at 25% afterward, with no unresolved QA pause and CSAT within two percentage points of baseline. Check response volume and ticket mix before interpreting CSAT. Revise if adoption fades with quality intact. Stop if quality or CSAT deteriorates. Treat baseline-only results as feasibility evidence.

## 8. Main risks

- Agents attach loosely related articles or misclassify tickets to reach the milestone.
- Writing slows resolutions, strains CSAT, or exposes customer information.
- Coverage falls after the reward because finding or maintaining articles still takes too much effort.
