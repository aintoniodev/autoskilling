## Four-week knowledge-base rally

Give each support team four weeks to earn a team meal during paid time. The team earns it when **35% of its resolved tickets have a useful knowledge-base article attached**, and the quality check passes. Keep the existing CSAT bonus as it is. Everyone on a team that reaches the milestone gets the reward, including agents who were on leave or handled few tickets.

Before launch, give a small group of agents across shifts and queues paid time to work through real tickets. Find out where search, permissions, or article publishing slows them down. Fix those problems, tell the team what changed, and give all agents paid practice time. Give agents protected time to write articles during the pilot.

### What counts

**A resolved ticket counts once when its final resolution includes a published article that accurately explains the fix or gives the next agent enough information to handle the same issue.** An existing article and a new article earn the same credit. Attach internal articles in the ticket’s internal field and share customer-facing articles only when appropriate.

Use existing ticket status, linked article IDs, and article publication status. Divide qualifying resolutions by all genuine resolved tickets, excluding existing spam, test, and merged-duplicate dispositions. A reopened ticket loses provisional credit and can count once when resolved again with a relevant article. Extra links earn nothing. A draft, a generic article, or a ticket resolved through the old workflow without an article earns no credit. When there’s no safe or useful article to attach, agents can resolve the ticket normally. It stays in the denominator. Test these cases against the report before launch.

### What people see

At resolution, show the agent a private confirmation such as “Article attached. This ticket counts toward the rally.” Show each agent their own progress on a rolling set of up to 20 resolved tickets, with a personal target of roughly one in three. A missed week or leave resets nothing. Post the team percentage and meal milestone weekly in the existing support channel. Managers see team totals only. Keep individual progress out of performance reviews and use existing records to calculate it.

### Timing and ending

- **Weeks 1–2:** Give all teams the workflow fixes and private feedback. Put article search and suggestions directly in the resolution flow, with an easy skip. Measure the rate after those changes using the same counting rule. The reported starting rate is 12%.
- **Weeks 3–6 and 5–8:** Randomly assign existing teams to the early or late start, balancing ticket mix and volume where possible. Compare the groups in weeks 3–4, when both have the improved workflow and only one has the reward. Each team gets four weeks to qualify.
- **Weeks 9–12:** End the rewards and keep the improved workflow and private feedback. Use the **share of resolved tickets with a qualifying article** in weeks 11–12 to decide what to do. At **35% or higher**, keep the workflow as standard. At **25% to under 35%**, revise it around the remaining friction. Below **25%**, drop the rally as an adoption tactic and revisit the workflow with agents. Compare the result with the weeks 1–2 baseline when interpreting the lift.

### What could go wrong

- **Irrelevant links, duplicate articles, or rushed resolutions.** Support QA samples 12 credited tickets per week across teams and queues. Three or more irrelevant, inaccurate, or unusable articles pause rewards for the affected teams pending review. Any exposure of customer data pauses rewards immediately for the affected team. The support ops lead owns the pause and restart. QA asks the knowledge-base owner to correct bad articles and support ops corrects wrong counts before awarding the meal.
- **The rally competes with CSAT.** Review existing team-level CSAT and reopen rates alongside adoption. If either deteriorates, inspect the tickets before resuming a paused reward or repeating the rally. Keep article attachment optional at resolution so agents can finish urgent or sensitive cases promptly.
- **Ticket mix makes the goal feel unfair, or use falls after the meal.** Review adoption by queue using existing team-level reports and bring recurring article gaps to the knowledge-base owner. Keep the workflow default and protected writing time after the reward ends. Budget one support ops owner under an hour a week for sampling, the team update, and routine count corrections.
