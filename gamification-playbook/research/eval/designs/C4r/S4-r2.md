# Knowledge-base adoption programme

Start from the current 12% of resolved tickets with an article. The goal is to make a useful article part of resolving every ticket where one applies, while preserving the existing CSAT bonus.

**Fix the resolution workflow first.** Give a small group of agents across shifts 30 minutes of paid time to close real tickets using the knowledge base. Ask where searching, linking or drafting adds time or creates risk. Use their feedback to put article suggestions in the resolution screen, make linking one click, and give agents a short draft template and a clear publishing owner. Give everyone paid practice time and share the fixes in the team channel under “You reported it, we fixed it.” If agents still have to choose between documenting a solution and keeping up with the queue, fix that before starting rewards.

**Reward a resolved ticket with a useful article.** Tell agents the rule in one sentence: “A resolved ticket counts once when it has a relevant published article or a complete new draft accepted by the knowledge-base owner.” Internal articles count where appropriate. Use existing ticket-resolution and article records. Two links on one ticket still count once. If a ticket reopens, reverse the provisional count and recheck it at its next resolution. A ticket closed through the old workflow counts if the article is attached by resolution. Agree on narrow exceptions before launch, such as spam and duplicate tickets, using existing dispositions. Check a few duplicates, reopens, old-workflow closures and exceptions before turning on rewards.

**Show progress and make the reward reachable.** At resolution, tell the agent which article counted or that their draft is pending review. Agents see their own progress in the support tool. Give each agent a personal goal of 40% of their eligible resolutions during the reward window, so the number of tickets needed follows their caseload. A missed week has no penalty. Post team progress in the existing support channel. When a team reaches 40% verified coverage over its four-week window, everyone on the team earns 30 minutes of paid time to use during a scheduled low-volume period. Managers see team totals only, and individual counts stay out of performance reviews.

**Run four reward weeks per team.** Give everyone the workflow fixes and feedback for two weeks first. Then start rewards two weeks apart across existing teams, assigning the start order before launch. Compare teams with and without an active reward during the overlap to see whether the reward adds anything to the improved workflow. Before the first reward window ends, make article suggestions the default in the resolution screen, with a quick way to record when no article applies. Keep the default and feedback after rewards stop.

**Decide on the lasting result.** Use one decision metric: the share of eligible resolved tickets with a qualifying article, corrected using the quality sample. Confirm the 12% baseline under the same definition. Measure again four weeks after each team’s reward ends. Keep the workflow and feedback if coverage is at least 40%. If coverage improves by at least 10 percentage points but stays below 40%, fix the largest remaining obstacle. If the gain is under 10 points, retire the incentive and revisit the workflow. Treat 40% as the first adoption milestone toward full eligible coverage.

**Watch for shortcuts and CSAT pressure.** Each week, the knowledge-base owner checks 10 randomly selected credited tickets for relevance, usefulness and customer data in drafts, plus five exceptions for misclassification. If two or more credited tickets fail, the support lead pauses rewards and the knowledge-base owner corrects counts and tells affected agents privately. Give drafts a review deadline of two business days and credit accepted drafts back to the original resolution date. Keep the CSAT bonus formula intact and watch team-level CSAT and resolution times for signs that agents are delaying closure or avoiding difficult tickets. If either deteriorates, investigate and fix the workflow before resuming rewards. Use an automated weekly report and budget under an hour for sampling, corrections and the team update.
