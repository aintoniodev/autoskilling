# Debate summary

Four debaters (theorist, empiricist, engineer, contrarian) attacked the eleven hypotheses
in `findings.md` over two rounds. Raw rounds are in `debate/`.

## Survived (consensus after round 2)

1. **Fix workflow friction first** (H3). Nobody disputed that a blocked or slower tool
   defeats any game.
2. **Verified, qualified credit** (H1 + H10). Credit finished work, reconcile against
   independent evidence, pause on harm. B for proxy response, D for the gate design.
3. **Attainable private progress with a next action** (H2), with easy re-entry after a
   miss (H5) and prompt confirmation (H9). A for goals and feedback, D for the exact
   prescriptions (n of N, timing).
4. **Purpose-limited access** (H8). Managers see aggregates; individual access needs a
   named purpose. A for monitoring risk, D for the policy.
5. **Measure after rewards end against a comparison** (H4 + H11).

## Added by the contrarian, accepted

- **Verified team progress against its own baseline, without names.** The private-only
  default made programmes invisible. Team bonuses without rank raised sales ~3% (Friebel,
  B); award cards ~12% (Kosfeld, B); surprise recognition 7.3% (Bradler, B).
- **Beneficiary impact** (H12): true stories of who the work helps (Grant, B).
- **Truthful norms** (H13): peer comparison letters worked as a package (Hallsworth, B).

## Killed as defaults

Named ranks, blanket manager access, calendar streaks, badge catalogues, instant
unverified prizes, loss framing, fixed brackets and habit clocks, mandatory confetti.

## Empiricist corrections applied

- Kerr and the bank enforcement case are D illustrations, not evidence.
- GitHub streak removal is B with code quality unmeasured; Stack Overflow badges C.
- Harkin d = 0.40 (trim-and-fill 0.19); Epton d = 0.34. Prompt confirmation and n of N
  are D prescriptions.
- Milkman: top lift +27%; the return micro-bonus added ~0.27 visits a week; 8% of 53
  treatments significant after withdrawal.
- Lally: 18–254 days among 39 good fits (C). "The one real-world study" removed.
- Ravid: 94 samples in total; no gain across 41, more strain across 23.
- Bandiera −14% / +24% graded B, not A.
- "Follow up for at least as long again" dropped: choose follow-up by volume (D).
- The one-mechanic cap is a D design choice.

## Engineer's shape for the skill

even 150 lines of workflow made designs too heavy; see `experiments.md`.
