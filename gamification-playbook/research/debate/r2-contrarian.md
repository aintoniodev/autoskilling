# Round 2 — Contrarian

**Kill the case bundle, not the reason to try.** It lost 7.3 points to no skill (62.6 vs 69.9); the rubric-free holistic read at comparable length also lost on launch (−8.5), employee notice (−3.2), and lasting value (−12.1). Four scenarios, repeated generations and two judges are not independent workplace replications. Low run spread makes rerolling a poor explanation, not this a universal result. The bundle failed even by my own salience criterion; none of these contrasts identifies an offending mechanic. I withdraw H7 as my second priority. Keep one noticeable payoff, not a stack of game parts.

## Rebuttals

- **Theorist.** Yes to aligning credit with the real outcome and independently checking harm (H1/H10); a replayable bad proxy is worse than no score. Yes to a supported cue, safe fallback and ordinary restart (H3/H5); don't make verification automatic just because starting can become routine. I reject putting goal/feedback seventh and compressing H6/H7 into “lightest feedback”: a lightweight receipt can be useful and still give no reason to try. Show what the worker or their team gained, before the next chance to act. Agree on separate audiences (H8) and conditional settlement (H9). Keep H11/H4 together, but a beautiful comparison and exit plan cannot rescue a treatment no one encounters.
- **Empiricist.** Agree H2 leads: goal/self-monitoring evidence doesn't validate public display, brackets or fixed milestones. Agree on downgrading mechanism-specific claims: incentive-window flexibility is not cue efficacy; a return reward is not a workplace streak; public stars aren't evidence for private recognition first; advance-bonus framing isn't a settlement trial. Rank data cut both ways; the furniture trial removed private rank under commissions, not a named board. I put immediately testable work value and safe, noticeable feedback ahead of withdrawal in the skill, despite better carryover evidence for H4. Keep the original rubric frozen and don't count repeated runs as new briefs. The holistic result makes rubric bias alone less plausible, not irrelevant; allow cash, social feedback and no game to win when well justified.
- **Engineer.** Agree on launch states, one operator and the cheapest independent validation; use a staffed correction route, not a new telemetry platform. I disagree with letting competent private n/N be the whole launch card: write the actual cue, the first credible payoff, and the return path for someone behind. A team huddle line or an anonymised example can be cheaper than a dashboard. “No prizes” is a valid finish, not “no motivation.” Keep restricted, actionable coaching available when warranted; a manager who cannot remove a blocker has no need for a name. Use cash/settlement machinery only when justified and approved. When spillovers or sample size sink H11, test feasibility without claiming lift.

## Updated priority for skill space

**1 H2** attainable goals and feedback; **2 H1** qualified outcomes; **3 H3** net workflow value and cue; **4 H8** purpose-limited visibility; **5 H12** show verified beneficiary impact; **6 H11** incremental comparison; **7 H4** reward-free follow-up; **8 H10** independent validation and replay; **9 H6** contingent recognition; **10 H7** bounded competition; **11 H5** re-entry without streak debt; **12 H13** correct an underestimated norm; **13 H9** honest, conditional settlement. Low space priority isn't permission to omit a safety gate.

## Five survivors

1. **Prove the work and the credit (H1/H10).** Define eligible, completed, excused and invalid work separately; independently reconcile a duplicate, reversal and omission. Name a quality/displacement stop and correction owner. No trustworthy evidence, no rewards; a no-stakes diagnostic can still run.
2. **Make a worthwhile attempt easy (H3).** Check a real work path net of QA/rework, put the cue there, and keep safe fallback and blocker ownership.
3. **Make progress reachable (H2/H5).** Show private n/N and a next eligible move; predeclare eligibility, account for case mix, and don't treat tiny samples as rankings. An absence or safe fallback neither earns credit nor blocks return.
4. **Make the payoff visible without exposing people (H8/H12; optional H6).** Show one shared milestone against the team's own baseline in an existing channel; give private, specific confirmation on the first qualifying action before the next opportunity. Example: “Last comparable week, 9 of 24 eligible handoffs met the standard. This week, 14 of 24 did; our goal was 16. On your next eligible handoff, use the checklist and confirm receipt.” Publish only verified, opportunity-adjusted counts, with case-mix changes disclosed and minimum worker/case coverage set before launch. Suppress small or inferable cells, including across releases; no drill-down, peer rank, collective penalty or personnel reuse. Stop the shared display if it induces rushing, blame or suppressed exceptions. If safe aggregation isn't possible, use a private, specific beneficiary example only if verified and consented, else simply name the concrete value in the work cue. [Grant](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.93.1.108) makes task significance a candidate, not proof that a quote or aggregate board drives adoption. Public recognition is opt-in without penalty, quality-linked, and never a list of non-recipients.
5. **Isolate the increment and test the exit (H11/H4).** On fresh briefs, blind-test no skill, the five-rule skill, and the same skill minus the shared-payoff prompt at the same output cap. Freeze the notice diagnostic (actual message, payoff, next move). In a workplace pilot, hold workflow, support and private feedback constant; vary only the signal and check reach, qualified first/repeat use, harm and cost. Predeclare scale/stop thresholds; measure new opportunities after settlement and withdrawal with support intact.

## Kills and open disagreements

Kill mandatory named leaderboards, full manager access and arbitrary brackets: exposure, helping costs and unequal opportunity. Kill calendar streaks, repair jackpots and streak graduation: pressure on absence and the wrong unit of progress. Kill badge catalogues, confetti, irreversible instant swag, default loss framing, fixed payout lags and universal “recognition before cash”: weak transfer or distorted contingencies. Keep prompt factual acknowledgement; separate provisional from final and never claw back wages if approved stakes exist. Kill H13 as core: a true norm may be weak, a strong-looking one may be incomparable, and [Hallsworth](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)00215-4) tested a package. Test only a verified, already favourable norm in a relevant, privacy-safe peer group.

**Still open:** I would require the payoff copy and test shared progress when safe; the others would accept private feedback without an added social cue. Whether verified beneficiary impact beats shared progress is unresolved; test one at a time. I still allow a time-boxed team contest as a later experiment, not core: comparable opportunities, reachable goals for laggards, genuinely reversible opt-in without disadvantage, no named bottom, rank-free comparator, and stop rules for quality, helping, selective work or bottom-tail harm. [Delfgaauw et al.](https://docs.iza.org/dp4395.pdf) justify testing, not expecting, a win. If it can't meet those conditions, omit it.
