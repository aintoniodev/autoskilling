# Round 2 — Engineer

**Replace the principle list with a short workflow that produces two things: what workers will see on their next real case, and what the operator needs to run it.** The 13-principle skill lost to no skill (62.6 vs 69.9); the rubric-free judge also preferred no skill on launch (71.2 vs 79.7), notice (52.8 vs 56.0), and lasting use (40.3 vs 52.4), at similar length. Rubric bias or verbosity alone is a poor explanation. Low repeat-run spread is not broad scenario coverage. The result rejects the bundle as written, not any individual mechanic. Checklist crowding is a hypothesis, not a finding.

## Rebuttals

**Theorist.** Agree on H1/H10: a deterministic replay of the wrong event proves nothing. Make independent reconciliation a launch gate, not a requirement to build an event store; a restricted, versioned export plus source spot-checks may suffice. Agree on H3: cue the supported work, not every queue entry. I move H2 above H11: otherwise we risk a well-controlled comparison of an offer nobody notices. Retain H11 with H4, but don't require causal identification to test basic feasibility. If assignment is not credible, don't use that pilot to justify scaling the game. Retained workflow support may explain persistence; don't call it automatic habit. Agree with folding H5/H9 into ordinary re-entry and conditional settlement.

**Empiricist.** Accept the evidence downgrades: gym bonus windows don't validate workplace cues; H2 doesn't validate a bracket formula; public stars don't prove private recognition beats cash; monitoring strain doesn't prove scoped coaching is harmful. I still put H3 first and H10 inside a hard gate: missing access or history can make a programme impossible whatever the effect size in the literature. Mixed rank results justify leaving H7 testable, not giving it more default space than the cheap things we can ship. Keep safety flags, score evidenced decisions rather than safeguard count, and don't retune the frozen primary rubric. Judge repeats improve repeatability, not the number of independent scenarios.

**Contrarian.** Right about launch salience: write the actual in-workflow invitation, first credible payoff, and next action if behind. Brief lift can buy real practice; it doesn't prove retention, and the practice still has to be worth its cost. H7's tournament and H6's public recognition studies justify candidates, not a named rank service: even top-only display can deter or expose people; check who can still win, who stops helping, and who opts out. An approved prize can offset costly trial effort; don't equate it with informative feedback. H9's fast confirmation is useful; shields and loss framing add state and disputes, so cut them. H5's next eligible action gives re-entry without a streak ledger. H8: agree on narrow coaching access only when someone can act on it; mentoring evidence doesn't justify dashboard access. H12 is cheap to try with a verified, consented example, not an invented customer benefit. H13 needs a current, comparable, non-identifying denominator and a genuinely misperceived norm; a bundled prescribing letter doesn't validate norm copy alone. Test ordinary copy first.

## Updated ranking and disposition

Ranked by deployment value per unit effort, not evidence grade. **Five core rules:**

- **#1 H3 — Make the safe path worth taking.** Use an existing workflow cue, verified benefit net of QA, and safe fallback.
- **#2 H1 + #5 H10 — Count qualified work and prove the count.** Separate target, credit and independent quality where needed; reconcile adversarial cases and catch displacement.
- **#3 H2 — Make the offer noticeable and attainable.** Show a credible first payoff, n/N and the next eligible action. Fold in H5's no-penalty restart and H9's prompt, accurately labelled acknowledgement.
- **#4 H8 — Limit access by purpose.** Private progress by default; narrowly approved coaching/corrections, no disciplinary reuse.
- **#6 H4 + #7 H11 — Learn, withdraw and decide.** Measure any increment over plain support separately from post-settlement persistence; otherwise label results feasibility-only.

Remaining ranks: **8 H5** (re-entry only); **9 H6** (recognition or prize optional, no universal ordering); **10 H12** (optional verified benefit story); **11 H9** (settlement branch only, never reclaim wages or vested rewards); **12 H7** (optional reversible peer test); **13 H13** (optional verified norm, omit if weak).

**Kill as defaults:** fixed milestones/brackets and habit clocks (not calibrated); named boards and blanket manager access (privacy, fairness and cooperation costs); streaks, repair bonuses and badge catalogues (new state, new exploits); instant unverified swag, fixed payout delays and loss framing (irreversibility or disputes); mandatory confetti, bespoke dashboards and nightly jobs (no proven increment, recurring cost). Useful feedback, reconciliation and independent harm checks survive without those implementations.

## SKILL.md: 120–160 lines, hard ceiling ~200

Frontmatter and scope → diagnose friction vs awareness vs real trial cost → draft the worker's first-use moment in an existing channel → build the operator card below → branch to **blocked**, **no-stakes feasibility only**, or **launch-ready once checks and approvals are evidenced** → choose plain support or justify one add-on → write the withdrawal and decision plan. Render participant copy and a short operator card, not a literature review. Mark unverified assumptions; don't claim access, approval or results.

Open `references/` only when needed: `evidence.md` for claim-level sources, grades and transfer limits; `operator-tests.md` for anonymised replay cases, permissions, corrections and settlement; `mechanics.md` for comparison cards and disqualifiers; `pilot.md` for assignment, spillovers, coverage, cost and readout examples.

### Minimum viable programme checklist

- [ ] One role, qualified work action and cue; one supported and one blocked walkthrough, including QA, fallback and blocker owner.
- [ ] First-use copy with what to do now, a credible employee payoff and visible confirmation; private n/N, reachable milestone, safe restart. Handle zero and uneven opportunities without retrospective exclusions.
- [ ] Frozen denominator, stable IDs, one credit at most per unit; shadow-check a typical case, a duplicate, a reversal and an omitted case against original evidence. No trustworthy history, no stakes.
- [ ] Independent quality and displacement checks, pause trigger and owner; limited views/retention and a staffed correction route. Where prizes exist, distinguish provisional from settled and specify late corrections.
- [ ] Named sponsor and operator, review volume × minutes plus a weekly cap, approvals and expiry. Use existing systems unless they fail a launch requirement.
- [ ] Baseline, equivalent plain-support comparator where credible, post-settlement opportunity window with retained support, minimum coverage and predeclared scale/change/stop criteria. Insufficient coverage is inconclusive.

**Open disagreements:** Is explicit first-use copy enough, or does one social or material mechanic add value? Test the same offer with only that element changed. Does a gate-first skill crowd out the offer? Compare the short workflow to no skill under the same budget and blind scoring; if it wins, ablate the copy step on fresh briefs. Track paired scenario deltas and safety flags without turning the holistic check into a revised primary rubric. Neither test is an employee outcome.
