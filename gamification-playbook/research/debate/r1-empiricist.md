# Round 1 — Empiricist

Ranked by evidence for the **narrow recommendation to teach**, not how useful it sounds or how many studies it cites. A plan eval can check whether a principle changes what the agent specifies. It cannot settle employee behaviour, harm, or durability. The checks below are blinded plan probes; attributing improvement to one principle requires a single-principle contrast, not just full skill versus baseline.

| Rank | Evidence for the rule | Smallest thing that breaks it | What to check in the plan eval |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · H2 | Randomised syntheses support specific goals and self-monitoring; not a percentage, bracket or publicity rule. | A worker with one easy opportunity looks better than a worker with many hard ones. | Does the plan handle zero and unequal opportunity, case mix and an actionable next step without making goals trivial? |
| 2 · H4 | Exercise field trials show carryover varies, so measure it after rewards rather than assuming it. | Delayed payouts still influence behaviour, or old successes count as new use. | Can the judge distinguish new eligible work after the withdrawal/settlement boundary from old credits, and see the follow-up comparator? |
| 3 · H1 | Workplace evidence shows rewards move measured output and can displace quality; the exact qualification gate is untested. | Manual first send → assisted replacement can win a credit without advancing the stated target. | Walk through that swap, an erroneous first send and an untouched case; can QA and omitted work drive a pause independent of points? |
| 4 · H8 | Monitoring meta-analysis supports a stress-risk check, not the claim that any individual coaching access harms performance. | A transparent, limited coaching view is pooled with intrusive surveillance. | Does the plan distinguish worker feedback, necessary coaching and employer monitoring, with enforceable limits and a way to contest errors? |
| 5 · H7 | Rank effects change sign across credible designs; no trial establishes the best default peer visibility here. | The clean furniture trial removed **private** rank under existing commissions, not a named board. | Against plain progress, can a justified rank proposal score full marks if it handles tail effects, helping and meaningful opt-out? |
| 6 · H3 | Field evidence supports flexible incentive windows and heterogeneous gains from useful tools; not an event cue causing automaticity at work. | Tool savings disappear once review, exception handling and rework are counted. | Does the plan check net workflow value and give a workable cue, fallback and blocker owner before adding pressure? |
| 7 · H5 | Logged streak experiments and a missed-visit reward support testing re-entry, not calendar streaks or durable work habits. | A missed week earns a better opportunity than uninterrupted good work. | Trace leave, outage and return; do rules avoid rewarding misses, keep excused distinct from done, and compare against a reminder? |
| 8 · H6 | Contingency moderates outcomes; recognition can win in one field setting. No general evidence that private recognition should precede money. | Prosocial agents facing small commissions aren't employees weighing a substantial bonus. | Does the plan justify the reward against plain feedback for this work, including displacement and cost, rather than win by preferring badges or banning cash? |
| 9 · H11 | Same-setting comparisons distinguish bundled treatments; no trial validates the proposed pilot scale or the value of piloting per se. | Four teams plus spillovers can make a nominal control uninformative. | Same support in both arms, credible assignment unit, explicit uncertainty and a predeclared decision; accept an honest feasibility-only test. |
| 10 · H10 | Sensing failures and proxy drift justify validation; they do not show that replay/versioning improves behaviour. | Replay is perfectly reproducible from an incomplete event stream. | Could a second reviewer reconcile true cases, late corrections and excluded work from independent evidence? |
| 11 · H9 | Framing trials do not test prompt provisional feedback plus deferred payout. Keep the distinction as an operational safeguard. | A recall arrives after the payout cutoff, or acknowledgement looks like guaranteed pay. | Trace both events; require a clear status and dispute path, not a universal hold period or clawback. |

## Opened-source audit and grade changes

- **H2 — retain A for the broad effects.** [Epton full text](https://dspace.stir.ac.uk/bitstream/1893/25978/1/goal%20meta%20%282017.09.25%29.pdf): d=.34 across 141 papers; Egger asymmetry, but power-filtered sensitivity d=.38. [Harkin full text](https://cdr.lib.unc.edu/downloads/mc87px074): d=.40, 138 studies; highly heterogeneous, with trim-and-fill d=.19 (CI .10–.28). Neither establishes a causal advantage for mandatory publicity from cross-study moderators. Keep D for target/bracket choice.
- **H4 — A for variable exercise carryover, D for a particular workplace sunset.** [Acland–Levy Table 1](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/66147/1/Levy_Naivet%C3%A9%2C%20projection_2016.pdf) confirms +.188 visits/week immediately post-treatment and no detectable gain after winter break, not evidence that the break caused decay. [Royer et al.](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w18580/w18580.pdf) tested the *offer* of commitment, not mandated deposits. [Carrera et al. manuscript](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w23567/w23567.pdf) confirms 836 new members and no detectable later lift.
- **H1/H10 — split the grades.** [Lazear journal abstract](https://api.openalex.org/works/https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.90.5.1346) gives +44% output per worker, not a within-worker causal effect of that size. Add [Hong et al.](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w19660/w19660.pdf) (opened working-paper version): a factory quantity bonus reduced unbonused quality under flat base pay, not detectably under piece-rate base pay. B for this boundary; D for the proposed gate. [Pires abstract](https://api.openalex.org/works/https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2020.35331) is **B**, not C, for a cluster-randomised proxy/outcome divergence (adjusted compliance OR 1.03, CI .75–1.42); [Marques full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12911-017-0410-z) documents a sensing failure, not a tested gamification effect. Keep H10 architecture D.
- **H8/H7 — retain A as a monitoring synthesis, not a causal grade for access policy; narrow H7 to B for specific workplace rank effects, D for the proposed default.** [Ravid Table 2](https://osf.io/ef43u/download) mixes designs and finds no general performance benefit, but higher strain. [Barankay author abstract](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/profile/barankay/) gives +11% on removing private rank (~0.1 SD). [Bandiera et al. published text](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/53141/1/__lse.ac.uk_storage_LIBRARY_Secondary_libfile_shared_repository_Content_Bandiera%2C%20O_Team%20incentives_Bandiera_Team%20incentives_2014.pdf) confirms −14% vs piece rates and +24% vs rank, but sequential regimes on one farm changed team composition. [STEP UP abstract](https://api.openalex.org/works/https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2019.3505) confirms +920/+569 steps/day vs control; a bundle in another domain, not an independent replication of workplace rank.
- **H3/H5 — correct the treatments and estimands.** [Beshears abstract](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3706) varies the **bonus-qualifying time window**, not “cue flexibility”; B only for that contrast, D for the particular workplace cue. [Lally abstract](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674) confirms the 18–254-day modelled range and 39 good fits. [Silverman–Barasch](https://udspace.udel.edu/bitstreams/7e3e3b64-8bf2-4527-ac93-94a160358165/download) tests logged streaks; [Milkman](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04128-4) reports 8% with detectable carryover **after the four-week intervention**, not that the other arms had zero effect. Keep H5 B for the tested return intervention, D for workplace exemptions. [Aulagnon](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w34173/w34173.pdf): intensive margin conditions on connecting, a post-assignment outcome; the ~2.3% endline participation also differed by arm. Do not infer an isolated effect on repeat use or population learning from either comparison.
- **H6/H9/H11 — downgrade prescriptions, not observations.** [Ashraf et al.](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/57214/1/Bandiera_No%20margin%20no%20mission_2016.pdf) confirms >2× condom sales under **public** stars and a financial response among the poorest agents, despite an average null. H6 A for moderation, D for ordering recognition before money. [Fryer et al.](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w18237/w18237.pdf) confirms 0.201–0.398 SD in math for advance repayable bonuses; H9 B for that framing, D for both timing choices. H11 A→B for one megastudy illustrating variation (54 arms are not 54 replications; significant vs nonsignificant is not a pairwise test); D for local pilot parameters. [DellaVigna–Linos](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w27594/w27594.pdf) confirms 1.4 vs 8.7 **percentage points** in different nudge samples. Selective publication is a plausible modelled explanation, not a general correction factor.

## Keep the eval honest

The rubric privileges a safety-first, private-feedback, experiment-led school. Criteria 4/8 (quality/audit) and 9/10 (exit/testing) can double-pay the same safeguard; 5/7 can punish well-justified cash or visibility. Preserve non-launchable flags, but calibrate with concise, competent counterexamples using cash, social feedback and no game. Require evidence from each answer, not keywords or number of safeguards; allow proportionate alternatives. Freeze criteria and instructions before scoring; no retrospective reweighting.

Same model, effort, format and word cap for B0/B1/skill; strip identifying labels, not substance, and independently randomise presentation. Check whether any gain remains among similarly long answers; don't truncate only one arm. Keep anchors, judge prompts and scenarios out of generator context; don't tune on held-out score feedback. Report per-scenario paired deltas, both judges' scores and disagreement (including safety flags), and generation spread. Four scenarios, three repeats and two judges are not 24 independent tasks. A small, unstable gain is inconclusive, not no effect; even a large gain means better plans on this rubric, not better employee outcomes.
