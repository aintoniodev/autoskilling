# Held-out scenarios (the eval set; not shown to research or debate delegates as targets)

S1. A 300-person field-service company wants technicians to complete a digital safety
    checklist in a new mobile app before each job (today ~35% do). Unionised workforce.
S2. A 25-engineer product team wants code reviews answered within one working day (today
    median 2.6 days). Engineers are sceptical of "gamification".
S3. A hospital wants nurses on three wards to log hand-hygiene moments in a new kiosk
    system; compliance is audited monthly by observers.
S4. A 60-person customer-support org rolled out a knowledge-base tool; agents should link
    or write an article on every resolved ticket (today 12%). Pay includes a CSAT bonus.

Prompt to the designing agent (identical for every condition, skill text appended or not):
"Design an incentive programme for this scenario. Cover what is rewarded, how it is
measured, what people see, how long it runs and how it ends, and what could go wrong.
Be specific. Max 900 words."
