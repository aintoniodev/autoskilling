# Evidence behind the playbook

Each claim in `SKILL.md`, with its source, grade and what it does not show. Grades:
**A** meta-analysis or several field RCTs, **B** one field RCT or strong natural
experiment, **C** lab or observational, **D** expert judgement, illustration or
postmortem. A grade describes the source design, not how well it transfers to your
workplace. Most of this evidence comes from exercise, sales, schools and public
services, not from adopting software at work. Treat every rule as a default to test.

Citations were checked against the paper, an author manuscript or an abstract record in
2026. The full research trail (findings, debate, experiments) is in `../research/`.

## What is not supported

- **Habit clocks** ("21 days", "8 weeks makes a habit"): contradicted by Lally (C).
- **Named leaderboards as a default**: field results on rank are mixed in sign (B).
- **Mandatory celebrations, confetti, badge catalogues**: no field evidence that these
  alone change work behaviour; badge effects are about steering activity (C).
- **Loss framing and clawbacks**: some school evidence of loss-framed bonuses (Fryer et
  al. 2012, NBER w18237), but fines can backfire and clawing back pay harms trust (D).
- **Exact bracket or threshold formulas**: no evidence for any specific number (D).
